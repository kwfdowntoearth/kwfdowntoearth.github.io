### Pioneer plants

In less managed spaces, pioneer species/ plants are resilient species that are the first to colonize barren environments. In human-managed ecological restoration, pioneer plants can be used to restore soil qualities for more demanding plants.

### Annuals

An annual plant is a plant that completes its life cycle – from germination to the production of seeds – within one growing season, and then dies. 

### Perennials

Perennial is used to differentiate a plant from shorter-lived annuals and biennials. It has thus been defined as a plant that lives more than 2 years.
