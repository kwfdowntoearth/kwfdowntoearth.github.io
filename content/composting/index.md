## what is compost 
"mixture of ingredients used as plant fertilizer and to improve soil's physical, chemical, and biological properties" - wikipedia

## why we compost
- improve soil fertility
- reduce dependence on chemical fertilisers 
- reduce waste in landfills (plant waste, food waste)

## what goes in? 
basically anything organic (which basically means derived from living things!)
- plant waste (cuttings) 
- animal waste (sadly, not so much at kwf)
- food waste
- coffee grounds (use sparingly, acidic)
